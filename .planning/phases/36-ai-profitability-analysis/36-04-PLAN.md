```
    </div>
```
Branches, in this precedence:
- `isLoading` → card shell (icon + title) with `data-testid="profitability-finding-skeleton"`
  wrapping two `<Skeleton className="h-4 w-full" />` and `<Skeleton className="mt-2 h-4 w-2/3" />`.
- `isError` → `<p data-testid="profitability-finding-error" className="py-8 text-center text-sm font-medium text-red-800">{ERROR_MESSAGE}</p>` inside the same card shell, no nested panel chrome.
- `finding === null` → `<div data-testid="profitability-finding-empty"><ChartEmptyState {...emptyStateCopy(incompleteCostData)} /></div>`.
- otherwise the finding body above.

Hard rules from the UI-SPEC, all load-bearing:
- The `SUGGESTED ACTION` eyebrow is `text-gray-700`, NOT `text-gray-500` — 12px/600 on
  `bg-secondary` needs the full 4.5:1 and gray-500 measures 3.98:1 there.
- Card chrome never changes with severity: no red border, no tinted card background,
  no severity-colored title. Only the chip changes.
- The narrative and action render VERBATIM — no truncation, no clamp, no "read more".
- The disclosure line is ALWAYS rendered and always last.
- `parseFloat` and `formatCurrency` must not appear in this file. AI prose arrives
  already formatted and grounded; a client-side reformat would break the audit trail.
- No button, link, popover, dialog or dismiss control — the card has nothing to tab to.
