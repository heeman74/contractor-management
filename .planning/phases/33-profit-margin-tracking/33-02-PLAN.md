Revenue-side model facts (verified 2026-07-27):
- `Invoice`: `job_id`/`trade_scope_id` nullable anchors, `discount_type` ("percent"|"fixed"|NULL),
  `discount_value` Numeric(10,2), `tax_rate` Numeric(5,2), `deleted_at`. Status check constraint is
  exactly `unpaid|partially_paid|paid` — there is NO draft state, so D-02 "all issued invoices"
  needs NO status filter.
- `InvoiceLineItem`: `invoice_id`, `quantity` Numeric(10,3), `unit_price` Numeric(10,2), `deleted_at`.
- `Quote`: same money fields plus `status` ("draft"|"sent"|"viewed"|"approved"|"declined"|"expired"|"revised"),
  `created_at`, and `project_id` (set on a project-level quote where job_id AND trade_scope_id are both NULL).
- `QuoteLineItem`: `quote_id`, `quantity`, `unit_price`, `deleted_at`.
- All Invoice/Quote `relationship()`s are `lazy="raise"` — column-only selects ONLY, never ORM rows.
