<objective>
Build the draft quote editor's AI surface: the trigger and its refusals, the AI
line anatomy (chip, marker, basis, per-line Accept), the unreviewed banner, the
card caption stack, and the regenerate confirmation.

Purpose: this is where SC2's guarantee is made visible — per-line review with no
rubber stamp, and an honest refusal when there is not enough history. Every
string here is byte-locked by 37-UI-SPEC; only the basis prose is AI-written and
it is rendered verbatim, never re-formatted.

Output: the notice, banner and sub-row components, the suggestions hook, the
wired line-items table, the tinted input row, the Trade column, and the
regenerate dialog.
</objective>
