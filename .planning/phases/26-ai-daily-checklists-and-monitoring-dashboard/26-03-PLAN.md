2. Create `web/src/lib/hooks/useDashboard.ts`:
   - `useDashboardProjects()` — TanStack `useQuery` with queryKey `['dashboard-projects']`, fetches `GET /api/dashboard` via apiClient, refetchInterval: 60000 (1 min polling).
   - `useDashboardAlerts(projectId?: string)` — TanStack `useQuery` with queryKey `['dashboard-alerts', projectId]`, fetches `GET /api/dashboard/alerts?project_id={projectId}` via apiClient, refetchInterval: 30000 (30s polling for alerts).
   - `useTradeTimeline(projectId: string)` — TanStack `useQuery` with queryKey `['dashboard-timeline', projectId]`, fetches `GET /api/dashboard/projects/{projectId}/timeline`, enabled only when projectId is truthy.
   - `useTradeTasks(projectId: string, tradeScopeId: string)` — TanStack `useQuery` with queryKey `['dashboard-tasks', projectId, tradeScopeId]`, fetches trade task detail endpoint, enabled only when both IDs are truthy.
   - `useMarkAlertRead()` — TanStack `useMutation`, POSTs to `/alerts/{id}/read`, invalidates `['dashboard-alerts']` on success.
   - `useAcceptRescheduling()` — TanStack `useMutation`, POSTs to `/alerts/{id}/accept`, invalidates both `['dashboard-alerts']` and `['dashboard-projects']` on success.
   - `useDismissAlert()` — TanStack `useMutation`, POSTs to `/alerts/{id}/dismiss`, invalidates `['dashboard-alerts']` on success.
