<objective>
Connect the evaluation engine to reality: evaluate the affected budgets whenever
a cost entry changes or a budget is edited, and sweep every budget nightly so
labor-driven spend (which grows on clock-out, with no cost-entry mutation to
hook) still crosses thresholds honestly (D-02).

Purpose: without these triggers, plan 34-03's engine only runs when something
calls it manually. This plan is what makes BUDG-03 automatic.
Output: three service hooks, an inline evaluation on budget edit, a sweep method
with the cron-required signature, and the 05:00 UTC APScheduler job.
</objective>
