<objective>
Derive labor cost from tracked time and ship the itemized category breakdown for
jobs, trade scopes, and projects.

Purpose: COST-05 (automatic labor derivation, including the keystone
success-criterion-2 guarantee that a later rate change never rewrites past labor
cost) and COST-06 (itemized costs per job / trade scope / project with category
totals). Also closes the RESEARCH Pitfall 1 gap: nothing currently stops a manual
cost entry from using the reserved `labor` category, which would double-count labor.

Output: three response schemas, four repository query methods, three service
methods, two new endpoints, an additively-extended rollup response, and the
integration tests that prove all of it.

**No migration.** **No new dependency.** **No per-entry rate query** — derivation is
exactly two bounded round trips.
</objective>
